# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Menu bar clipboard history for text, links, images and files
- Card grid with search, type filters, pinning and click-to-copy
- ⇧⌘V floating panel over any app, with a customizable shortcut
- Optional auto-paste into the current app (needs Accessibility)
- Instant QR Share for links and short text
- Ignored apps (password managers by default), Pause, and auto-delete after a day, week or month
- History size from 100 to 2,500 items; launch at login
- iCloud sync support for builds signed with an Apple Developer profile
